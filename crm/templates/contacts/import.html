How it works
  • Export from Excel, Google Sheets, Gmail, Outlook or another CRM as CSV.
  • The file needs an Email column. These are also recognised: First name, Last name, Name, Phone, Company, Title, Stage, Source, Tags, Notes.
  • Contacts that already exist (same email) are updated, not duplicated.
  • Only import people who agreed to hear from you.
